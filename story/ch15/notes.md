# Chapter 15 - Notes

- Story: ./chapter15.md

## Battle Atmosphere

Reduce elaborate similes selectively without stripping the battle's sensory presence. The author specifically wants Magmar's flames casting writhing shadows across the walls retained; the comparison of static to thousands of tiny needles can be shortened. Preserve light, heat, accumulating power, and suspense between attacks, not just the move sequence.

## Electabuzz's Windmill Motion

The windmill comparison specifies Electabuzz's actual fighting motion; retain it rather than treating it as ornamental imagery. The Platinum Pokedex entry describes arm-windmilling to strengthen punches, and Ultra Sun describes the circular motion building electrical charge. See [Electabuzz's Pokedex entries][electabuzz-dex]. This supports the chapter's repeated arm rotations and charge buildup, not a requirement to add new misses or escapes.

[electabuzz-dex]: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Electabuzz_(Pok%C3%A9mon)#Pok%C3%A9dex_entries

## Smokescreen Visibility

The author chose uncertain action: Smokescreen successfully conceals both combatants while Electabuzz is already charging. Amber hears the contact and sees light through smoke, then sees Magmar stagger out. Do not narrate a precise uppercut or chin graze through the concealment, or have the punch blow the screen away.
