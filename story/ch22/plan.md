# Chapter 22 Plan: Viridian Passage (Working Title)

**POV:** Amber (1st person)
**Timeline:** Immediately after Chapter 21
**Location:** Route 1 arrival corridor and Viridian City

## Chapter Purpose

Give the story geographical breathing room between the playful Mew encounter
and the larger Route 2 Rocket operation. Establish Amber and Kyle as temporary
companions before Alonso enters, and let Viridian function as a real city
rather than an aftermath destination.

## Settled Sequence

1. Amber and Kyle finish Route 1 and enter Viridian together.
2. They remain together because both are continuing north.
3. Their new dynamic gets ordinary space through practical city needs such as
   healing, food, lodging, supplies, registration, or route information.
4. Amber cannot explain Mew's interest. Kyle may be more focused on the fight
   or on Mew's strength.
5. They leave Viridian toward Route 2 before encountering Teddiursa, Alonso,
   the larger Rocket operation, or the Mystery Dungeon.

## Viridian Gym Status

The old plan in which Giovanni awarded Amber and Alonso discretionary badges
after the Dungeon is removed. Whether Amber or Kyle visits, challenges, sees,
or postpones the Viridian Gym during this passage remains open. Do not restore
the development-campus tour or long administrative wait.

## Constraints

- No Teddiursa or Alonso before Route 2.
- No large Rocket operation inside Viridian.
- Do not turn this into another exposition chapter about Mew or Rocket.
- Do not formalize Amber and Kyle as permanent companions yet.
