# Chapter 16 - Notes

- Story: ./chapter16.md


## Battle Register and Focus

Chapter 16 uses a panoramic register, not Amber's restricted viewpoint. It can
follow both fronts and briefly enter Fuji's memory or Oak's thought without
turning the whole chapter into either man's close-third narration. See
[battle style][battle-style] and [Oak's scene presence][oak-style].

The battle starts with extracting the children and preventing pursuit, then
becomes a struggle to buy time while the neighborhood evacuates. The standoff
gives Oak and Fuji space to argue over honor, the war, and scientific failure;
Ditto's response belongs in that exchange. After the injection, keep the two
fronts legible: Tauros against half-Magmar in the street, Arcanine and Exeggutor
containing Ditto-Dragonite in the yard. Their support and attacks interact.

## Preservation Anchors

- The opening meteor comparison gives Dragonite's arrival catastrophic weight
  before its reveal. Preserve that scale rather than reducing it to an impact.
- Keep the physical weight of Tauros's grapple and Ditto's resistance, not only
  the move names. Ditto's clean transformation and Magmar's painful mutation
  need enough description to register separately.
- Oak's unsoftened landing and survival of Overheat establish unusual
  durability. Do not explain its limits or replace the landing with a roll.
- Keep Oak's failure to recognize Fuji, the forgotten grant ceremony, and the
  argument's role in buying time. Do not restore superseded recognition or
  Mega Evolution clues.
- Keep the silence around Oak's apparent defeat and the wait after Giga Impact.
  Ditto's reversion and refusal remain observable without a stated motive.
- Oak checks Magmar before the final look at the syringe. Do not add
  an explicit diagnosis or moral explanation to that ending.
- Hyper Beam grazes Tauros before Oak recalls it, accounting for its burnt
  hide on reappearance. The recall prevents a full hit, not all injury.

## Staging

- Tauros's ram during Overheat dislodges half-Magmar from the first Earthquake's
  trap, leaving the final Earthquake a renewed immobilization.
