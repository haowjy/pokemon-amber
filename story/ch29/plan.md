# Chapter 29 Plan: Heracross (Working Title)

**POV:** Amber (1st person)
**Timeline:** Continues the prepared Dungeon expedition from Chapter 28
**Location:** Known off-route Mystery Dungeon near the Pewter approach

## Chapter Purpose

Pay off the trio's first deliberately chosen Dungeon expedition with a
memorable Heracross encounter while advancing Amber, Kyle, and Alonso from
crisis allies into companions who voluntarily take on problems together.

## Settled Heracross Direction

- Heracross is confident enough to charge opponents stronger than it.
- It is overpowered, gets back up, and charges again.
- Amber's eventual capture is a conventional conscious-battle capture rather
  than a Caterpie-style peaceful choice or Teddiursa-style custody promise.
- Fighting offense makes Heracross relevant to the coming Pewter challenge,
  but Bug/Fighting does not make it a Grass Pokemon.

## Working Sequence

1. The Dungeon expedition reaches a situation that reveals Heracross's
   reckless confidence rather than presenting it as a convenient encounter.
2. Heracross attacks, challenges, or repeatedly interferes with the group.
3. Amber recognizes useful strength but must respond to the individual in
   front of her rather than treating it as a Rock-counter purchase.
4. Kyle and Alonso contribute to the wider Dungeon situation without taking
   the capture away from Amber.
5. Heracross remains conscious for the successful registration attempt.
6. The expedition finds an exit or a stable stopping point after the capture.

## Required Reconciliation Before Drafting

- Amber has a four-Pokemon allowance after Giovanni's Viridian badge and is
  currently planned to carry Ditto, Caterpie, and Teddiursa, leaving the
  working fourth slot for Heracross.
- Teddiursa's exact capture/registration moment must be settled before prose.
- Exact battle choreography, moves, Dungeon inhabitants, and exit mechanics.

## Constraints

- Do not restore the deleted replacement-Pidgey/release sequence merely to
  manufacture an open slot.
- Do not make Heracross another instantly devoted emotional-choice capture.
- Do not let the capture erase the unresolved Ursaring rescue.
