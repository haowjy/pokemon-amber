# Chapter 27 Plan: Route 2 Recovery / Dungeon Proposition

**POV:** Amber (1st person)
**Timeline:** After Chapter 26, while the group remains on Route 2
**Location:** Route 2 camp and the approach toward Pewter

## Chapter Purpose

Let Amber, Kyle, and Alonso recover from the forced Mystery Dungeon and choose
to continue together. Alonso may introduce a future prepared Dungeon
opportunity, contrasting planned exploration with the collapse they just
survived.

## Working Sequence

1. Amber, Kyle, and Alonso make camp or reach a safe Route 2 rest point after
   Giovanni leaves.
2. They process practical consequences: injuries, the missing Ursaring,
   Teddiursa's unresolved demand, the Pidgey lost during the chase, and
   Giovanni awarding badges to Amber and Alonso but not Kyle.
3. Alonso explains that entering a known Dungeon deliberately is different
   from falling into an unmapped one. Prepared entries use route knowledge and
   safety equipment such as Escape Orbs.
4. Alonso knows of a more appropriate Dungeon opportunity on the way toward
   Pewter. Amber sees possible team-building value before facing a Rock Gym.
5. Kyle's presence is included in the choice. This is the first opportunity
   for the three travelers to continue by mutual decision rather than crisis.
6. The chapter ends with agreement to travel together and investigate the
   prepared opportunity. Chapter 28 owns preparation and entry; Chapter 29 is
   the working Heracross payoff. Do not require a formal declaration of
   permanent companionship.

## Heracross Direction

The prepared Dungeon is the working capture site for Heracross. Chapter 28
owns preparation/entry and Chapter 29 owns the encounter/capture direction.
Its exact ecosystem and choreography remain open. Heracross is Bug/Fighting,
not a Grass Pokemon.

## Removed Legacy Dependencies

- Amber does not catch a replacement Pidgey in a now-deleted post-Viridian
  chapter.
- No Pidgey release is required here.
- Giovanni has awarded Amber and Alonso standard Viridian badges; Kyle did not
  receive one. Amber therefore has a four-Pokemon active-custody allowance
  under the current licensing plan.
- The Grass-oriented Dungeon, roster-space problem, and Heracross capture must
  account for Amber's current Ditto, Caterpie, and Teddiursa custody state.

## Constraints

- Do not immediately repeat a full Dungeon crawl merely because Alonso offers
  one.
- Do not minimize the failed Ursaring rescue.
- Do not treat Giovanni's attack and retroactive test explanation as
  friendship or sponsorship.
- Keep the trio's continuing companionship chosen but informal.
