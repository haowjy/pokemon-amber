# Chapter 21 Plan: Kyle and Mew (Working Title)

## Placement and Function

Immediately after Caterpie, still on Route 1, before Amber and Kyle reach
Viridian:

- Introduce Kyle through action instead of an Oak errand.
- Make Kyle Amber's temporary companion before Alonso joins.
- First show Rocket through a few field grunts rather than the full operation.
- Put Mew on-page without resolving the later Mew-search mystery.
- Seed Mew's extraordinary curiosity about Amber without explaining her body.

## Working Sequence

1. Amber and Caterpie continue along Route 1.
2. Kyle is traveling north after visiting Oak to receive his likely second
   partner, Riolu. His established childhood partner is Mankey.
3. An unusual disturbance leads Amber to Kyle, already confronting a few
   Rocket grunts.
4. The grunts are trying to pursue, corner, or contain Mew. They are not the
   competent core of the later cave operation.
5. Amber and Kyle cooperate when the grunts escalate. Kyle is direct and
   forceful; Amber reads the field and adjusts.
6. Mew treats the confrontation as a game, dodging, teleporting, imitating,
   startling, or redirecting attacks without behaving frightened.
7. Mew repeatedly abandons easy escape opportunities to inspect Amber. It may
   copy Amber, Ditto, or her gestures; the exact business remains open.
8. Mew leaves when the game stops being interesting. Nobody captures,
   defeats, directs, or receives an explanation from it.
9. At least one withdrawing grunt notices Mew's unusual reaction to Amber,
   creating a small Rocket surveillance seed.
10. Kyle and Amber continue in the same direction provisionally. Do not
   formalize the permanent trio yet.

## Mew Direction

- Playful, curious, mischievous, and casually beyond everyone present.
- Extremely interested in Amber, not merely friendly to every child.
- Its curiosity may ultimately relate to Amber's trace Mew-derived biology,
  Ditto's Mew-derived nature, or both, but this chapter provides no diagnosis.
- Mew does not deliver lore, select Amber as a chosen one, or join her.

## Constraints

- Keep this brief relative to the later Rocket cave mini-arc.
- Do not combine it with Teddiursa or Alonso's introduction; both belong on
  Route 2 after Viridian in Chapter 23 onward.
- Do not reveal Rocket's full command structure, search apparatus, or the
  Mystery Dungeon here.
- Do not make Mew struggle to escape ordinary grunts.
- Mankey is Kyle's established first partner. Riolu is the likely Oak-arranged
  second partner, but its exact placement details remain open.
