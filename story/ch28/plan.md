# Chapter 28 Plan: Prepared Dungeon Entry (Working Title)

**POV:** Amber (1st person)
**Timeline:** After Chapter 27
**Location:** Route 2 / Pewter approach and a known off-route Mystery Dungeon

## Chapter Purpose

Contrast a deliberate, prepared Mystery Dungeon expedition with the forced
Route 2 collapse. Show what Alonso normally does before entering one, establish
the cost and limitations of safety equipment, and let Amber and Kyle actively
choose the danger this time.

## Working Sequence

1. Alonso identifies the known Dungeon opportunity and explains what is known
   about its entrance, ecosystem, and risk level without claiming certainty.
2. The trio obtains or checks required supplies, including an Escape Orb. The
   current working price remains ₽300, but exact mechanics remain open.
3. Rangers, posted rules, another adventurer, or the maintained entrance can
   distinguish this regulated site from the hidden Dungeon beneath Rocket's
   cave.
4. Amber considers the coming Pewter Rock Gym and possible team needs, but she
   does not enter with a guaranteed species shopping list.
5. Kyle participates in preparation rather than simply following Amber and
   Alonso. The three trainers choose to enter together.
6. The Dungeon establishes a distinct environment and first obstacle. Do not
   force the Heracross capture into the same chapter unless pacing demands it.

## Open Decisions

- Exact Dungeon theme, rating, inhabitants, layout, and duration.
- Whether it is Grass-oriented, forest-oriented, or another ecosystem that
  plausibly supports Heracross.
- Escape Orb mechanics and whether the group carries one or backups.
- Whether a Ranger inspection or permit check appears on-page.

## Constraints

- This is a chosen expedition, not another accidental collapse.
- Do not treat regulated Dungeons as safe or fully mapped.
- Do not call Heracross a Grass Pokemon.
- Do not assume Amber already has a first badge or a fourth custody slot.
