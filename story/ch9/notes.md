# Chapter 9 - Notes

- Story: ./chapter9.md

## Identification Rationale

- Erika sends Amber with Mary because Amber alone saw the culprit's face during the earlier collision.
- Mary initially tries to keep Amber out of the Game Corner for her safety and Ditto's safety. Amber's ability to recognize the culprit is the practical reason Mary relents.
- Chapter 9 leaves the culprit unnamed; the details of her brother's situation emerge in Chapter 10.

## Revision Constraint

- Amber takes Mary's hand before entering the Game Corner and keeps hold through the shop entrance and identification. When the girl flees, Mary lets go and tells Amber to stay. Amber follows anyway with Ditto's occupied Pokeball in hand; she does not release Ditto.
- No coffee or caffeine subplot in this chapter; keep Amber's reactions grounded in the surroundings and investigation rather than stimulant-driven behavior.

## Character References

### Kayla
Original character (OC):
- First appears dozing off during planning session with Erika
- Dismissed by Erika when exhausted ("zombie shuffle toward the exit")
- Characterized by apocalyptic yawns

### Erika
Based on Erika from the Pokémon games ([Bulbapedia][bulbapedia-erika]):
- Gym trainer working toward becoming Gym Leader (not yet official leader)
- Uses a pager/communication device
- Assigns Mary to investigate Oddish theft
- Suggests solving the case could improve Mary's standing with the board

### Mary
Based on Cooltrainer Mary from Celadon Gym ([Bulbapedia][bulbapedia-celadon-gym]):
- Currently suspended from official gym battles
- Assigned to investigate Oddish theft
- Has Bellsprout partner
- Leads investigation through Celadon's shops and Game Corner

### Kaede (Unnamed in Prose)
Based on Kaede from the manga "How I Became a Pokémon Card" ([Bulbapedia][bulbapedia-kaede]):
- Childhood friends with Erika, the Celadon Gym Leader
- Severely allergic to pollen, flowers, and Grass-type Pokémon in general (hence constant sneezing around Oddish)
- In the original manga, she's a member of Team Rocket (though Erika is oblivious to this)
- Has an Oddish that later evolved into Gloom in the manga
- Often pictured sneezing, looking tired, and clutching her runny nose
- In this chapter: caught stealing Oddish, chased through Game Corner, captured by Mary's Bellsprout
