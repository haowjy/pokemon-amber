# Chapter 9 - Summary

Amber accompanies Mary as an eyewitness during the item-shop inquiry, identifies the thief near the Game Corner, and helps pursue her before Mary's Bellsprout makes the capture.

- Story: [Chapter 9](./chapter9.md)

## What happens:
### 1. Planning the Investigation:
*   Kayla, a drowsy security staff, is dismissed after a planning session with Erika, a gym trainer pursuing the Gym Leader position.
*   Erika reiterates Mary's suspension but implies that solving the Oddish theft could help her case.
*   Erika tells Mary to take Amber because Amber is the one who saw the girl's face. Mary, frustrated but determined, focuses on investigating item shops for clues about the stolen Oddish.

### 2. Department Store Disorientation and HP UP Inquiry:
*   Amber and Mary visit the Celadon Department Store, an overwhelming experience for Amber who is accustomed to the pixelated version.
*   They navigate the bustling store, encountering a traffic-directing Mr. Mime and a Delibird hauling a returns cart.
*   On the fifth floor, dedicated to vitamins, Mary inquires about HP UP stock. The clerk confirms normal supply but dismissively recalls a girl he assumes is from the Factory Ward asking what HP UP does, how to make it, and about improving someone's health.
*   Amber notes the high price of official HP UP (₽9,800 per bottle) and the absence of ingredient lists, leading her to question the regulatory standards of the Pokémon world.

### 3. The Game Corner and a Crucial Lead:
*   After visiting several more shops, Mary tries to take Amber back before checking the less-safe Game Corner, concerned about a child with a rare Pokémon. Amber insists that the girl could pass Mary unnoticed; Mary relents because Amber is the necessary eyewitness.
*   They navigate the chaotic, neon-lit Game Corner, filled with promoters, gamblers, and a Grimer blocking the street.
*   At shop number twelve, they overhear a conversation between a man and a sneezing girl that confirms she stole the Oddish believing its spores could be used for HP UP to help her brother; her name and her brother's situation remain unknown.

### 4. Pursuit and Capture:
*   Mary bursts into the shop. Amber recognizes the girl and tells Mary, "That's her." The girl flees towards a back alley, leaving a trail of golden spores.
*   Mary releases Amber's hand and tells her to stay. Amber follows anyway, holding Ditto's occupied Pokeball; her small size helps her pass Mary in the cluttered shop, but her shorter stride hinders her pursuit.
*   Mary's Bellsprout successfully trips the girl with a Vine Whip, causing her to fall and drop a cloth bag, releasing more golden spores.
*   The girl, in tears, pleads not to be sent to jail, explaining her brother's urgent need for help.

## Potential speculation:
*   **Mary's Redemption Arc:** Mary's determination to solve the Oddish theft, despite her suspension, and her concern for Amber, suggest she is a character driven by a strong sense of justice and responsibility, potentially leading to her redemption and reinstatement.
*   **HP UP Controversy:** Kayla's near-revelation about Oddish no longer being used for HP UP, combined with Erika's immediate cover-up and the thief's belief in Oddish's importance, hints at a hidden secret or scandal surrounding the Celadon Gym's HP UP production and its ingredients.
*   **Economic Disparity:** The high price of HP UP and the thief's desperate act for her brother's sake highlight a significant economic disparity in the Pokémon world, suggesting that access to vital resources like medicine may be limited to the wealthy, driving some to desperate measures.
*   **The Thief's Motivation:** The girl's plea about her dying brother reframes the theft as desperation rather than corporate espionage; the details of his situation remain unrevealed.
*   **Amber's Developing Empathy:** Amber's observation of the thief's desperation and her internal conflict over the situation suggest her growing empathy and a deepening understanding of the complex realities of this new world, moving beyond her initial gamer's perspective.
