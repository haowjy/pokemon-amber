# Chapter 26 Plan: Route 2 Exit / Giovanni's Test

**POV:** Amber (1st person)
**Timeline:** Immediately after Chapter 25's Mystery Dungeon traversal
**Location:** A secondary Route 2 forest exit

## Chapter Purpose

Close the Route 2 incident with Giovanni violently testing the witnesses and
then using his legitimate Gym Leader identity to retroactively disguise the
attack. Reward Amber and Alonso while making Kyle's poor contribution and
exclusion a sharp personal failure.

## Settled Sequence

1. Amber, Kyle, Alonso, Teddiursa, and their Pokemon emerge through a secondary
   Route 2 exit after the Mystery Dungeon.
2. Giovanni is waiting outside. He does not introduce himself or ask questions
   first; he immediately releases Nidoking and attacks/tests the group.
3. Amber and Alonso contribute most meaningfully to defeating Nidoking. Kyle
   participates little or performs poorly relative to what he expects of
   himself. Exact battle choreography remains open.
4. After Nidoking loses, Giovanni pauses. His initial reaction can briefly read
   as displeasure or recalculation before he changes presentation.
5. Giovanni reveals that he is Viridian's Gym Leader and plays the unexplained
   attack off as an impromptu Gym test.
6. He awards standard Viridian Gym badges to Amber and Alonso for passing.
7. Giovanni pauses before Kyle but does not give him a badge. He need not
   openly humiliate Kyle; the omission is enough.
8. Giovanni leaves without bringing anyone back to Viridian. There is no tour
   and no prolonged wait.
9. Kyle is devastated and furious with himself. Amber and Alonso received
   recognition in a confrontation where he believes he should have excelled,
   sharpening the pressure he already carries from Saffron.

## Giovanni's Actual Purpose

Giovanni is assessing the trainers who emerged from the compromised Rocket
site and testing whether they are dangerous. His Gym Leader identity supplies
plausible deniability only after they defeat Nidoking. The badges make the
cover story materially credible and let him exit without admitting any Rocket
connection.

He does not behave reluctantly, introduce himself as a civic responder, or
conduct a polite witness interview before attacking.

## Badge Consequences

- Amber and Alonso receive legitimate standard Viridian badges.
- Kyle receives no badge because his contribution did not satisfy Giovanni.
- Amber's first badge raises her active-custody allowance to four Pokemon under
  the current licensing plan, which can support later roster decisions.
- The award is not an ACE certification or a special badge class.

## Constraints

- Giovanni is never seen beside Rocket personnel and never admits knowledge of
  the cave operation.
- He attacks with Nidoking before revealing his identity.
- Kyle's exclusion must register emotionally; do not smooth it over with an
  immediate consolation prize.
- No Matori tour, development-campus sequence, long cleanup wait, or return to
  Viridian.
