# Pokemon: Ambertwo

A fanfiction about an isekai'd adult consciousness inhabiting Dr. Fuji's clone,
Amber. Grittier and more realistic than canon; draws on the anime, games and manga.

Chapter prose lives in `story/chX/`; read [story/AGENTS.md](story/AGENTS.md) before
working there: which file to edit, how to compile, what to load. The KB (wiki,
`styles/`, craft `issues/`, `research/`) is outside the repo: find it with
`meridian context kb` (`$MERIDIAN_CONTEXT_KB_DIR`), never a repo-local `wiki/` or
`.meridian/kb`. KB pages are kebab-case (`amber-mc.md`).

## Writing style

**Rhythm.** Vary sentence length, structure and paragraph shape to follow the
emotional movement of the scene and the impact of each beat, on purpose rather than
as a pattern.

**Purposeful prose.** Every word, line, action and paragraph serves the narrative,
tone, character, chapter or current beat; cut anything without a specific purpose.
Record useful line-level reasoning in HTML comments in `chapterX.annotated.md`.

**Humor** is deadpan: describe ridiculous situations in the same serious,
matter-of-fact voice as everything else and let the incongruity carry it. Never
announce, explain or emphasize the joke.

**Hedging.** Cut habitual qualifiers (`probably`, `kind of`, `maybe`) unless the
uncertainty itself matters.

**Dialogue.** Distinguish speakers by their priorities, information choices, syntax
and actions under pressure, not by repeated verbal tics or stock gestures. Use an
action beat when the action changes the exchange, reveals information or reorients
the reader.

**Tonal range.** Under pressure or grief: shorter sentences, concrete sensory detail,
restrained figurative language. Lighter scenes may run longer and more playful. Let
tone shift in the prose without announcing it.

**Section breaks** are kaomoji, such as `\[@.@]/`, `\[^.^]/` or `-[?.?]-`, where a
visible scene break is needed.

**Punctuation.** Em dash `---`; ellipsis `...` (no Unicode); plain ASCII `"` and `'`;
italics for names, emphasis and emotionally charged direct thought.

**Spelling.** `Pokeball`, `Pokedex`, `Potion`, `Pokemon`.

## Links in repo docs

Inside the repo, use relative Markdown links. Never link a local path outside it,
including the KB: link the GitHub URL instead, for the KB
`https://github.com/haowjy/writing-kb/blob/main/pokemon-amber/kb/`. Prefer
reference-style links (`[shortcut]` in the text, `[shortcut]: https://...` at the
bottom).

## Talking with the author

Be harsh and critical when evaluating ideas, and support each critique with specific
examples; search the web if needed.

When planning future story content, answer worldbuilding and mechanics questions
directly, present options without advocating for one, and ask clarifying questions
before building on an idea. Don't invent character dialogue or psychology unless
asked, suggest plot beats or scene structures unprompted, or build scenarios from
assumptions. Ask "What tone do you want for the confrontation?" rather than writing
"Here's how the confrontation should go...".
